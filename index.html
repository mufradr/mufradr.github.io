Saved worlds

Three manual slots for different histories. Saving finishes the current year and pauses; loading resumes paused. Replacing a manual slot keeps its previous save.

Autosave backups

Autosaves capture completed years. Export important worlds: private browsing, clearing site data or iOS storage cleanup can remove local slots. Save files use .worldengine. Share/setup links still restart history.

World

Links and setup files reproduce the starting world, seeds and rules in this engine version. They do not save progress or edits made during a run. Imported maps must be shared as a setup file.

Each visit starts a new random history unless you load a world from Saves. The same world, history seed and rules always give the same history. Change only the history seed to see which outcomes are structural and which were luck.

Import a world

A greyscale equirectangular heightmap, such as a Draggable World export. Set the elevation that pure black and pure white stand for. Draggable World's WCL export uses −11,000 and +9,000 m with sea level at mid-grey.
