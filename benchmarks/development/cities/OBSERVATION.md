# Pre-final browser observation

The first final batch passed 212 automated tests and 1,230 prepared scenario
years, but its desktop early-world browser checkpoint reported a populated cell
without a valid people. The measured working-tree runtime was
`3ddc66d17636f63777debf2b0fa0b8eac8da0965181562488f39a0d8171e8498`;
the Git revision in that manifest is the unchanged pre-commit warfare parent.

The older mixing cleanup removed a minority ID in provinces below one resident
while retaining its nonzero share. A later emigration could make that orphaned
share the largest group and swap the missing ID into the primary slot. That
state could subsequently grow past one resident. Keeping valid fractional
communities until actual extinction, and clearing shares with no minority ID,
fixes the cause. Regression coverage includes the small direct example and a
260-year ordinary-start browser-seed run with integrity checks every twenty years.

The failed observation and its associated pre-fix measurements are retained as
`observation-before-fix-*`. They are **not** the final checkpoint's successful
verification, timing or runtime identity. The main manifest, tests, scenarios,
performance.json and browser.json were rerun after the fix and the small hot-path
cleanup; their matching runtime hashes identify the final implementation.

The pre-fix CPU sample showed a 19.31% developed-world time increase versus
dev.3 and a 16.58% compressed-save increase. Final measurements are reported in
README.md. Both samples are incremental prepared-workload observations rather
than cumulative compliance with the original v0.16 allowance.
