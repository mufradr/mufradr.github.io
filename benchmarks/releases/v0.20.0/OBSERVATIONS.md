# Integration observations

The source-pinned dev.4 pilot completed Earth seed development-01: 1,718 settled
owners, 59 ever-empire state IDs (none ended), and no half-area decline among twenty
eligible states. Its [raw result](pilot-dev4.json.gzip) and
[manifest](pilot-manifest.json) preserve that evidence. The frontier/concentration
correction addresses this defense lock without scripting regional winners.
Two additional dev.4 pilot worlds completed during investigation; their raw
results and pilot-summary.json are retained separately. They are not pooled with
the v0.16 comparison or performance figures.

An early full-suite attempt reported a difference against the retained historical
political-loop fixture. Another attempt reported a difference against the old
technology/exit-option implementation. These are comparisons of alternative
implementations, not a recorded failure to reload a save. Retained evidence is
in observation-reference-compression.log and reference-diagnostics.log; subsequent
checks add per-year RNG/typed-array comparisons and setup equivalence. All 219
checks passed in the final full suite. The precise cause of the earlier
intermittent comparison divergence was not isolated; do not present the later
pass as proof that the original v0.16 same-process reproducibility observation
has been resolved. Isolated repeated measurements and an independent full-history
replay provide separate checks of the release engine.

Genuine v0.13/v0.16 and all four development saves retained exact continued
values/RNG. Raw encodings after further entity creation differed in graph alias
topology for immutable static metadata, a distinction already made in the save
unit tests. legacy.json records both results. Mutable technique aliases remain
validated on installation; the late-world replay separately checks an immediate
load/re-encode and continued values.

The original v0.16 archive and all four development checkpoint archives are
unchanged. Concurrent historical/pilot/prepared scenario wall timings do not
supply the release CPU claims. Performance cases are run separately with adjacent,
alternating before/after processes and retained raw distributions.

The low-driver (quiet) control still changes ownership/state counts: disabling
war declarations and crisis sensitivity does not disable every legacy succession,
nomad-formation or abandonment path. Its speed ratio is not evidence that the
10% allowance is met on identical topology. Two attempts to construct a fixed
ownership control stopped at their explicit warm-up hash assertion; their logs
are retained as observation-fixed-control*.log and no accepted timing result was
produced. That experimental runner was discarded. The headline CPU comparison
uses the unchanged, evolving prepared early/developed fixtures; their differing
final state counts remain explicit. No frozen topology is claimed.

The paused render fixture was updated to supply actual urban population for its
3,290 synthetic cities, matching the new selector’s input contract. The final
render.log was rerun after that fixture correction; it is a current stress scene,
not a pixel/timing comparison with the old province-based city selector.

Trailing whitespace in the retained console excerpt was normalized for Git checks;
reported assertion values and digests are unchanged.
